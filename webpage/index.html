Metadata-Injection

Sample pages for the metadata injection plugin. The two players play the live stream, send a test JSON event to the plugin's API and show the event as it arrives: as ID3 tags over HLS, or on a data channel over WebRTC. The publish page sends your camera over WebRTC and injects events on a data channel instead of calling the API.

HLS / ID3 player

Plays the HLS stream and logs the injected events as they arrive in the stream as timed ID3 tags.

Open HLS player

WebRTC / Data Channel player

Plays the stream over WebRTC (WHEP) and logs the same events as they arrive on the metadata data channel. Requires Wowza Streaming Engine 4.12 or later.

Open WebRTC player

WebRTC / publisher

Publishes your camera over WebRTC (WHIP) and injects events by sending them on a data channel instead of calling the API. Needs HTTPS for camera access.

Open WebRTC publisher
